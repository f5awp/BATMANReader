# DX Trader — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 14, 2026**

DX Trader ("the app") is an independent scheduling and shift-trade assistant for
shift-based dispatch employees. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is
stored, and your choices. DX Trader is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official
product of any airline or employer.

## Who we are

DX Trader is built and maintained by an individual developer. There is no company server,
no analytics vendor, and no advertising network involved. If you have questions about this
policy, contact: **ervinlee.umich@gmail.com**.

## The short version

- We do **not** run our own servers. Your data lives in **your own iCloud account**.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
- Sign in uses **Sign in with Apple** — no password, no email harvesting.

## What the app stores, and where

DX Trader uses Apple's **CloudKit** to store data in your personal iCloud account. Two
scopes are used:

**1. Your private iCloud database (only you can read it):**
- App preferences and settings
- Personal notes
- Your trade history and standing trade intents

**2. A shared iCloud database used to coordinate trades between coworkers:**
- Your Employee ID and display name (so coworkers can identify who a trade is with)
- Trade requests and responses you send or receive
- Posts and replies you make in the in-app channel
- Your published trade preferences (e.g. which shifts you are open to trading)

This shared data is only meaningful to other people using the app within your work group.
It is stored in Apple's iCloud infrastructure, governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Sign in with Apple

Signing in uses **Sign in with Apple**. The app receives an anonymous Apple user
identifier used to associate your account with your roster line. You may choose to hide
your email via Apple's private relay; the app does not require your email address.

## Information you provide

To link your schedule, you enter your **Employee ID**. This is used only to match you to
the correct roster line and to identify you to coworkers in trades. It is not shared
outside the app's iCloud data.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app uses Apple Push Notification service to alert you to
trade offers, replies, and mentions directed at you. You can turn notifications off at any
time in iOS Settings.

## Calendar access (optional)

If you choose to add shifts to your calendar, the app requests permission to write events
to your device calendar via EventKit. This is entirely optional and used only when you
initiate it. No calendar data is read or transmitted.

## What we do NOT collect

- No advertising identifiers
- No location tracking
- No third-party analytics
- No browsing history
- No contact list access

## Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives in your own iCloud, you control it. You can delete trade history,
notes, and preferences from within the app. Signing out and deleting the app removes local
data from your device. To remove shared data associated with your account, delete your
trades and posts within the app before signing out. You may also manage app data through
your device's iCloud settings.

## Children

DX Trader is intended for working professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and the app
will surface the update.
